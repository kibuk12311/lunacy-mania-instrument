# Changelog

## 0.9.4

- **Skin tab — an osu!mania 4K skin editor and builder:** a seventh tab, «Скин» / "Skin" (short labels; the tab bar still fits one line at 320 px, at 260–220 px a smaller font, and it hides in compact mode as before). A skin comes from three places: **osu!'s Skins folder** (where tosu says osu! stable is, else `%LOCALAPPDATA%\osu!\Skins`; only read — the skin is copied into a working copy in userData\skin-work), an **.osk / .zip** (picked or dropped; every name checked, a zipped folder loses its folder, size capped) or **from scratch** with the builder. The last eight skins are listed to reopen (userData\skin-recent.json); older working copies are removed.
- **Editor:** the [Mania] section with `Keys: 4` of skin.ini as the osu! wiki documents it, in groups with plain ru/en labels and tooltips (the skin.ini key in each): General (Name, Author); sizes and positions (ColumnStart, ColumnRight, ColumnWidth — one slider for all columns and a box each —, ColumnSpacing, ColumnLineWidth, HitPosition, LightPosition, ScorePosition, ComboPosition, BarlineHeight, WidthForNoteHeightScale, LightingNWidth, LightingLWidth); behaviour (UpsideDown, KeysUnderNotes, JudgementLine, NoteBodyStyle, SpecialStyle, ComboBurstStyle, KeyFlipWhenUpsideDown, NoteFlipWhenUpsideDown, SeparateScore, LightFramePerSecond); colours (Colour1–4 and ColourLight1–4 per column, ColourColumnLine, ColourBarline, ColourJudgementLine, ColourKeyWarning, ColourHold, ColourBreak, with opacity where the key has it); the pictures of every column (KeyImage#, KeyImage#D, NoteImage#, #H, #L, #T as thumbnails, below); the stage (StageLeft, StageRight, StageBottom, StageHint, StageLight, LightingN, LightingL, WarningArrow) and the judgements (Hit300g … Hit0). skin.ini is read and written line by line (src/skin/skinIni.js): what isn't edited comes back byte for byte — comments, spacing, a BOM, CRLF or LF, other sections and other key counts, a non-UTF-8 file kept as its bytes; a `//` comment after a value stays. Undo / redo (a slider drag is one step).
- **Builder:** note shape (circles, bars, arrows that point their column's way, diamonds), column colours with presets (1-2-2-1, 1-2-1-2, one colour, four colours; six colour sets; a colour picker per column), dark or light, column width, note size, hit position, hold bodies (solid, striped, faded; stretched or repeated), keys pressed and not (plain, with an icon, coloured), the stage (no edges, lines, lines and glow), judgements as MAX/300/200… or PERFECT/GREAT… in a bold, thin or pixel font of its own, column light and hit flashes. Every picture is drawn by the app (src/skin/art.js, font.js — no third-party art) at @1x and @2x, written as PNG with node's zlib. On an opened skin it redraws only the parts ticked (notes, holds, keys, stage, lighting, judgements, sizes and name) into a new folder, so undo still has the old pictures; the export takes only the ones skin.ini points at.
- **Your own picture for every element:** every picture of the 4K part — per column the note, LN head, body and tail, the key and the pressed key; the stage's left, right and bottom, the hit-line picture (StageHint), the column light, the hit flash and the hold light, the start arrow; each judgement (MAX/300g, 300, 200, 100, 50, miss) — has a thumbnail: a click («Своя картинка…» / "Own picture…") or a file dropped on it takes a PNG, JPG or WebP, which the tab turns into two PNGs with their transparency (the @1x and an @2x twice its size; a file named …@2x is the @2x and the @1x is made half of it; the @2x kept within 4096 px), written as lunacy-mania\custom\<key>-<time>.png and @2x.png in the working copy and set in skin.ini; «Сбросить» / "Reset" goes back to the picture the skin had when it was opened (or the builder last drew), «Применить ко всем колонкам» / "Apply to all columns" does either for the four columns at once. Checked in main (PNG pair, @2x exactly twice, at most 4096 px and 12 MB); a very large picture is warned about, and in a skin made from scratch so is a note, LN end or key that is not square (Interlude fits it into a square). The preview shows it at once, the .osk carries it, and the Interlude .isk of a skin made from scratch uses it in place of the drawing (fitted into the square frame, a hold body stretched, a hold tail turned over for Interlude's upscroll-first tails; the hit-line picture as the judgement line, the stage edges, column light and flashes switched on when they are the user's). Judgement pictures have no place in an Interlude noteskin; combo and score digits are [Fonts] pictures, not part of [Mania], and are not edited.
- **Live preview:** osu!'s 640 × 480 stage drawn from the skin's own pictures and values — columns at ColumnStart with their widths, gaps and lines, keys (pressed by an autoplay), notes and holds (NoteBodyStyle) falling to HitPosition, the hint and judgement line, column light at LightPosition tinted with ColourLight#, hit flashes and hold light, the judgement at ScorePosition — on a short built-in loop at 170 BPM; downscroll / upscroll, scroll speed. Every edit shows at once.
- **Export:** «Сохранить .osk и открыть в osu!» / "Save an .osk and open it in osu!" writes Documents\Lunacy mania\скины\<name>.osk (never over an older file: "name (2).osk") and opens it, so osu! stable and lazer import it; «Сохранить копию папкой» saves a plain folder copy there. The original skin is never written to.
- **Interlude** (only for a skin made from scratch — an osu! skin is finished in Interlude's own editor, and Interlude can import an .osk itself): a `.isk`, Interlude's own skin package — skin.json and a Noteskin folder with noteskin.json and grid textures (`note[1x4].png`, `holdhead`, `holdbody`, `holdtail`, `receptor[1x8].png`, and with lighting `receptorlighting`, `noteexplosion`, `holdexplosion`; `stageleft`/`stageright`, `judgementline`), re-implemented from Prelude's format (MIT): square frames, one row per column colour, ColumnWidth × 1080/480, enums by name, colours ARGB. Dropped on Interlude's window it is copied into its Skins folder; the app doesn't write there. Interlude has no key images (receptors instead), takes the hit position, scroll direction and judgements from its own settings and ruleset, and has no per-skin judgement pictures in the noteskin.
- **Dan by rate:** the ladder on the analysis card steps by 0.05 (0.9×, 0.95×, 1.0× … 1.5×) instead of 0.1.

## 0.9.3

- **Local profile and the Profile tab:** a new «Профиль» / "Profile" tab (and «Создать карту» shortened to «Создать» / "Create" so six tabs fit 320 px; the tooltip keeps the full name). Its «mania-tracker» page has the tracker player (the same loader as Picks), their dan, four tiles, MinaCalc ratings, history chart, recent plays and a link to mania-tracker.com; «Мой профиль» / "My profile" is a skill profile learnt from your own plays, all on this PC (userData\local-profile): every play that reaches the results screen in osu! stable or lazer is recorded (fails and quits are kept but don't count), and the history can be imported once from osu! stable's scores.db (read only, matched by md5 to the Songs folder, resumable) and from Interlude's scores (no accuracy there: weaker evidence); lazer's own history can't be read (realm). Each play gets a performance dan — the map's dan at its rate, full at 96.5% accuracy, +0.08/% above (at most +0.25), −0.2/% down to 93%, −0.35/% below, −0.1 per 1% misses — attributed to the patterns and axes by the map's pattern shares (tech 40%, long maps partly Stamina); each axis and pattern is the 0.95^i-weighted mean of its best 20 maps (old plays fade 0.5 dan a year, warm-ups left out), shown with ± and confidence once 3 maps support it; overall = the mean of the four axes. Daily history chart per axis and pattern, best and recent plays, export / import / reset, «Записывать мои игры». Picks gets a third source, «Мой профиль», used by picks, the plan, the auto-marathon, «для тебя» and Progress; its plays also feed "hide played". Model and constants: docs/local-profile.md.

## 0.9.2

- **Interlude support** ([YAVSRG/YAVSRG](https://github.com/YAVSRG/YAVSRG), portable). An **IL** button in the analysis footer opens a panel: the Interlude folder (picked, found in the usual places or unlinked; it needs `Songs\charts.db`), saved in userData\interlude.json. Interlude has no API for what is selected or played in it (its only outside channel is a named pipe that takes "focus" and "version"), so the running game is read by a bundled reader (below).
- **Interlude's library:** `Songs\charts.db` and `Data\scores.db` are SQLite in WAL mode that Interlude may have open, so they are copied (with their -wal) into userData\interlude-cache when they change and the copies are read; Interlude's folder is never written to (except `Data\collections.json`, below). The panel searches the 4K charts by artist, title, difficulty name, creator and pack; a click puts the chart on the card (dan, patterns, MSD, the rate ladder) until osu!'s own selection changes. The chart is the original .osu Interlude kept in `Songs\.origins` when its id still matches, else an .osu made from the binary chart (notes, holds, BPMs and scroll speeds, re-implemented from Prelude); it is copied with its audio and background into Documents\Lunacy mania\interlude, so the Rate tab, remap and Marathon's «+ selected» work on it.
- **Chart ids:** Interlude's SHA-256 chart id and its osu! → Interlude conversion are re-implemented exactly (float32 sums, round-half-to-even), checked against Prelude's own test map; maps we have are found in the library by their .osu MD5 (the osu! origin), by that id or by beatmap id.
- **«→ Interlude» / "→ Interlude"** on rate copies (one or a batch), marathons, generated maps, remaps and Picks downloads: an .osz in Documents\Lunacy mania\карты, Interlude brought to the front through its pipe (or started), the .osz shown in Explorer to drop onto Interlude's window — Interlude only imports what is dropped on it. Rate copies of an Interlude chart are sent that way by themselves. Nothing goes into `Songs` or charts.db.
- **Playlists in Interlude:** the marathon list (each map at its rate), the Picks plan and the favourites can be added to `Data\collections.json` as a playlist (rates kept to 0.5–3.0, two decimals) — only while Interlude is closed (checked in the process list; it rewrites the file on exit), after a copy to `collections.json.bak-<date>`, next to every folder, playlist and like already there, under a name of its own. Maps not in the Interlude library are listed.
- **Picks:** with a folder linked, "hide played" also hides osu! maps played in Interlude (its scores joined to the charts' osu! origins).
- **Interlude live — the card follows the game:** `interlude-reader.exe` (`native/interlude-reader`, C# on ClrMD: `OpenProcess` + `ReadProcessMemory` only, types and fields found by name, no offsets, no writing, no injection, no network) is started by the main process while «Follow Interlude live» is on — by default whenever a folder is linked (userData\interlude.json keeps `live` and `allowDownload`). Its JSON lines are read line by line (app/interludeReader.js); it is started again after a crash with a growing pause (1 s, 2 s, 4 s … 60 s, back to 1 s after a minute of running), stopped by closing its stdin and killed on quit if it doesn't exit. `--allow-download` is passed only when «Скачать служебный файл .NET, если его нет» / "Download .NET's helper file if it's missing" is ticked (off by default); a change restarts the reader. A selection (chart hash, rate, mods) is put on the card after 0.3 s without another change (scrolling the wheel doesn't analyse every chart passed): looked up in the linked library (the copy of charts.db read again once if the hash isn't there), copied out as with a picked chart and analysed **at Interlude's rate**; a chart the library doesn't have is shown by its title, artist, difficulty and rate from the reader with a note. **Priority with osu!:** whichever selection changed last has the card — the selection controller gives the card back to osu! when osu!'s own selection changes, and a new selection or rate in Interlude takes it again (app/interludeLive.js).
- **Interlude live — the footer and the IL panel:** the footer badge reads «Interlude · live» while attached (and the IL button turns green, amber on a problem); the panel has the two checkboxes and the reader's state in words, ru/en: waiting for Interlude, attached (its version), `field_not_found` → «Interlude обновился? Сканер не нашёл часть данных (…)» with the rest still working, `dac_not_found` with the hint to tick the download setting, `attach_failed` (an elevated Interlude), a missing reader exe (antivirus), a crash with its restart time.
- **Interlude live — the overlay:** the reader's play frames (30 a second) go into an overlay session of their own (src/overlay/interludeData.js): judgements in the ruleset's own names — six (SC, Wife3, osu!mania) fill the six rows in order under short names (MARV, PERF, …, the ratio as MARV:PERF), any other count shows the ruleset's own rows — and Interlude's own accuracy; KPS and the key lines from Interlude's real per-note events (press time = note time + delta) and held columns (holds end on release or when the key is up); UR from the hit deltas; NPS from the chart's notes in the library; the play id starts a new play on a retry; the score screen is the results (one opened from level select shows nothing). The same visibility gate as for osu!; the widgets show whichever game is playing.
- **Overlay — no longer gone after minimizing:** minimizing a window (osu!, the game in borderless, Win+D, or the app's own window, pinned 📌 or not) could leave the overlay windows minimized or hidden while the overlay still thought they were up, so they never came back. Now a widget meant to be on screen is restored and shown again as soon as Windows minimizes or hides it, the app's window minimizing, restoring or being shown re-raises them, and while any widget is up a light watchdog (every 1.5 s) re-asserts the top-most level and the z-order (a borderless game taking focus can push itself above top-most windows); also after sleep and display changes. The widgets have no parent window and are never tied to the main window's visibility; the main window's own always-on-top stays below the overlay's screen-saver level.
- **Building:** `npm run build:reader` builds the reader (needs the .NET 8 SDK); the release ships `resources\interlude-reader\` (interlude-reader.exe ≈ 12 MB, the bundled .NET 9.0.22 DAC ≈ 1.3 MB) through electron-builder's extraResources, and the build stops if they are missing. THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES lists ClrMD and the .NET DAC (MIT).

- **Your skill by hand, without mania-tracker:** Picks' «Вручную» / "Manual" is now a profile editor — your dan for Speed, Jack, Stamina and Tech (the four axes mania-tracker rates), each on the usual dan + step picker, an optional name, and "Copy from mania-tracker" as a starting point when a tracker profile is loaded. The overall dan is the mean of the four, the way mania-tracker's own overall reads (its 13.66 in our fixture is exactly the mean of its four skillsets). It is remembered with the Picks settings and used everywhere a tracker profile is: picks per pattern and goal (stream → Speed; jumpstream, handstream, stamina → Stamina; chordjack, jack and their kinds → Jack; tech → Tech), the custom range, the training plan, the auto-marathon "from my Picks profile" and "For you" on the analysis card; the summary line reads «TestPlayer · общий дан Delta low (13.65) · вручную». Played maps come from mania-tracker or a linked Interlude folder: with a hand-set profile and no tracker profile, "hide played", the plan and the auto-marathon's "skip maps I've played" leave out the osu! maps played in Interlude once its folder is linked, and without either the checkboxes are off and say what they need; Progress needs mania-tracker and says so. The old single manual dan is folded in: settings from 0.9.1 open with that dan on all four axes.
- **Picks → «Сессия» / "Session": a practice session from the maps you already have**, nothing to download. The page lists the 4K osu!mania charts in osu! stable's Songs folder (where tosu says it is, else the one seen last, a folder picked with "Songs folder…" or stable's default place; read only — an .osu whose size and time did not change is not read again, and only its head until it is known to be 4K mania; 7K, osu!standard and the app's own rate copies and marathons are left out) and in a linked Interlude library (an Interlude chart that is the same .osu as a Songs map is one map with both sources). osu! lazer keeps its library in a realm database nothing outside lazer can read, so its maps are not included, and the page says so. Every chart is analysed once in the background line (dan, main patterns, length; the selected map's card never waits), remembered in userData\session-library.json and picked up where it stopped on the next start; a new app version analyses again while the old results stay usable; Pause / Resume, and sessions can be built while it runs. The session: length 20 / 30 / 45 / 60 min or any 10–180, 1–3 focus patterns (the Picks chips, General / Detailed), the goal (comfort / growth / challenge around your dan for the pattern, from mania-tracker or the hand-set profile), "allow rates" (a map at the rate that reaches its place's dan, picked on an estimate and then checked by a real analysis from 1.0 towards it, 0.85–1.4×, slow-downs only a little and last) and "skip maps played in the last 7 days" (mania-tracker plays and Interlude scores). It runs warm-up (a few maps from about a dan below up to the window, the focus and related patterns) → main block (the goal's window with a small wave of dans; with several focus patterns they take turns, every fourth place may be a related pattern) → push (one map above the window, two from 45 min) → cool-down (one shorter, easier map), about as long as asked; no map twice, another difficulty of a song already in it only last. Each map shows its part, title and difficulty, dan, pattern, length, rate and where it is (osu! / Interlude); ↻ on a map takes another one for that place (a little further from its dan when nothing else is that close), "↻ Whole session" builds it again. Sessions can be saved (userData\sessions.json) and opened again, copied as a text list, exported as an osu! collection (collection.db in Documents\Lunacy mania\коллекции with the collections already in it kept and the old file kept as collection.db.bak; a map at a rate is made as a rate copy next to the map first, like the Rate tab; Interlude-only maps are listed as skipped), as an Interlude playlist (each map at its rate, through the existing safe path) or as one marathon .osz (Interlude charts copied out first). No maps found, too few for the length, or none near the target: the page says which and points to rates and to Picks.
- **Past the top of the scale:** a dan above Kappa high reads «выше Kappa · 21.3» / "above Kappa · 21.3" everywhere (card, rate ladder, "For you", Picks, plan, marathon, Create, Rate, mania-tracker progress past its table), and just «выше Kappa» where the number already stands beside it; the READMEs say Alpha…Kappa (there is no Omega).

## 0.9.1

- **Overlay — NPS window:** a fourth widget, "Map NPS": the notes per second of the map itself (not of the presses) at the play position, the map's peak, and a graph of the whole map with a moving playhead (the played part tinted). Hold notes count once by their head; DT, HT and rate copies read in real time. All five styles, opacity, size and position, toggles in the tray and the settings window, made-up numbers in the preview.
- **Overlay — steadier KPS graph:** the rate is a triangular 0.8 s window sampled on a fixed 100 ms grid, so finished samples never change and late judgements barely move it; the newest point slides between grid points, the y-scale eases towards a ceiling above the play's max instead of jumping each frame, and the big "now" number is eased and holds its integer.
- **Overlay — no flicker in song select:** the windows go through a gate with hysteresis: shown only after the play state has lasted 0.4 s with its clock moving or judgements coming (1.5 s of play state alone is also trusted), hidden only after 0.3 s without it; a shown window gets the current snapshot before it appears. The results screen works as before.
- **Overlay in the main window:** an OV button in the analysis footer opens a panel with the overlay on/off, a checkbox per widget, "Move windows (Ctrl+Shift+O)" and a link to the overlay settings; it shares overlay.json with the tray and the settings window and follows every change. The tray menu gets a checkbox per widget too.
- **Create map — volume:** a «Громкость» / "Volume" slider (0–100 %, remembered) under the preview's scroll speed sets the song and the hit clicks together.
- **osu! lazer support:** tosu v4 reads lazer too, and the app now works with it. The footer names the osu! that is connected (stable / lazer). The selected map is read from lazer's hashed file store (tosu's `/files/beatmap/file` and `/json/v2` paths); nothing is ever written there. Rate copies and remaps are made from a copy of the map in Documents\Lunacy mania\lazer and handed to lazer as one .osz that it imports as a separate set (the charts get BeatmapSetID:-1 / BeatmapID:0, so lazer never replaces the original set); Marathon's «+ selected in osu!» uses that copy; the auto-marathon skips the Songs lookup and downloads instead. Lazer's own speeds (DT/HT at any speed, Rate Adjust) are the rate on the card, in the Rate tab and in the overlay. Collections: lazer can't import a collection.db, so with lazer the export also writes a list of the maps with links and says so. The overlay's key lines use lazer's real key counters, and "osu! updated?" has its own lazer hint.
- **Overlay — a single odd frame no longer restarts the play:** a non-play state must last 0.5 s (the results screen still ends a play at once) before the KPS max and the lanes start over. The key counters of tosu's precise feed are now used even though tosu v4.26.3 sends no clock with them.
- **Portable file name:** the portable build is now `Lunacy-mania-Portable-<version>.exe` (the installer stays `Lunacy-mania-Setup-<version>.exe`).

## 0.9.0

- **Model picker for Create map:** "Classic" — the proven model from 0.8 — stays the default and is what Auto runs. Next to it, experimental models to try: XGBoost v3, and the neural nets S (fast) and M; the neural net L (needs a graphics card) shows as not there yet — it is still training and comes in a later update. On 179 held-out songs the new models hit the asked dan more often, but their notes and patterns are less like a human mapper's, so each one says so in its hint and none is the default. A card names the model it was made with, and if the one you picked couldn't run (no file, or no sound breakdown) it says which one was used instead.
- **Ratings:** 👍 / 👎 on every variant, with up to four reasons ("too jacky", "fun", …); your ratings are counted and can be exported to a file.
- **Candidates and the critic:** the "Candidates to choose from" slider (1–20, default 1: off) makes several candidates per variant and lets the critic pick the most human-like, fun and comfortable one; the card shows its scores.
- **Stems and repeats:** the song is split into kick, snare, hi-hat and harmony when it loads; with that, repeated parts of the song can get similar patterns ("Repeats get similar patterns"), and a mapper style can be picked when the model knows any.
- **Graphics card path:** the neural nets run on the graphics card (WebGPU) when there is one that passes a self-check, else on the CPU; "Regenerate only this part" redoes one part of a variant in place.
- **Updates:** the app checks for a new version again every 4 hours while it runs (installed and portable), not only at start.

## 0.8.0 — first public release

- **Pattern kinds:** one «General / Detailed» switch for every tab. In Detailed, Create map (and a part's own patterns) offers 21 kinds of the six patterns — rolls, trills, minitrills, running man, jumptrills, split trills, anchors, dumpstream, chord trills, dense and jumpy handstream, handjacks, jumpjacks, quadhand, quadstream, minijacks, longjacks, gluts, speedjacks, coordination and jacky stream — spread over the song's parts or mixed every few bars; variants are ranked by how much of the chosen kinds came out. Result cards, part lines, the analysis card and its pattern strip show kinds; Picks and the auto-marathon add quadstream, handjack and speedjack.
- **Installer with auto-updates** next to the portable exe. The installer updates itself from GitHub Releases; the portable version tells you when a new one is out.
- **Create map v3:** BPM and offset detection for songs with several BPMs, 1/3 and 1/6 snaps, song parts with their own dan and patterns, a playability guard, several ranked variants, a preview player with a playfield and a timeline, locking a part and regenerating the rest, mapsets and dan ladders in one click, rate versions, metadata, preview point and background, and remapping an existing map.
- **Analysis:** MSD, Sunny ★, a choice of calculator (ours, Mixed, Daniel, Azusa, Roxy, Companella or all), a difficulty line along the map, patterns by part, "for you" and a dan-by-rate ladder.
- **Rate:** rate for a target dan, several rates at once, mirror and no-LN versions, presets.
- **Marathon:** an automatic marathon from your skill profile, course templates and a difficulty curve.
- **Picks:** maps for your skill from mania-tracker by pattern and goal or your own dan range, one-click download, a training plan, similar maps, favourites exported as an osu! collection, and progress.
- Russian and English interface, a new icon, and the name Lunacy mania (the project: Lunacy mania instrument).
